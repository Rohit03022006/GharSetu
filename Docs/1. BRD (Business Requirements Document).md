## 1. Purpose of This Document

This BRD defines the **business case, objectives, scope, and stakeholder requirements** for the Real Estate Management & Property Discovery Platform. It answers *why* the platform should be built and *what business value* it delivers - the PRD (companion document) answers *how* it will be built technically.

## 3. Business Problem / Opportunity

### 3.1 Problem Statement (Business View)

The Indian real estate discovery market is served by fragmented, low-trust tools:

- **Buyers** waste time across multiple portals, cannot easily evaluate affordability or locality quality, and have no reliable way to trust reviews or listing authenticity.
- **Brokers** run their entire sales pipeline informally (WhatsApp, spreadsheets), losing leads and having no visibility into what's converting.
- **Builders** cannot see which of their listings perform well or why, and have no standardized way to get verified/trusted on a platform.
- **The Market Itself** has no dominant player enforcing listing quality - fake, duplicate, and outdated listings are common and erode buyer trust industry-wide.

### 3.2 Business Opportunity

A platform that **owns the full journey** - discovery -> financial qualification -> comparison -> visit -> negotiation -> booking -> post-sale review - creates:
- Higher buyer engagement time (financial tools + comparison + reviews keep them from bouncing to competitors)
- A defensible trust moat (verification + moderation + verified reviews - hard for low-effort listing sites to replicate)
- A natural expansion path from a Sale-only or Rental-only tool into a **combined marketplace**, capturing more of each user's real estate lifecycle (e.g., a renter today may become a buyer in 2-3 years, on the same platform)

## 4. Business Scope

### 4.1 In Scope
- Multi-role platform: Admin, Builder, Broker, Buyer, Guest
- Property listing management (Sale and Rental)
- Search, discovery, comparison, and financial planning tools for buyers
- Lead management and sales analytics for brokers/builders
- Admin-driven verification, moderation, and platform-wide analytics
- Booking/visit scheduling with availability management
- Reviews & ratings tied to verified transactions
- Notifications (in-app, email) using open-source/non-paid channels
- Property sharing to external channels (WhatsApp, social, etc.)

### 4.2 Out of Scope (for current phase)
- Actual payment processing / transaction settlement (platform facilitates connection and scheduling, not money movement)
- Legal document generation or legally-binding e-signatures
- In-house loan origination or bank partnerships (Finance Suite is estimation-only, not a loan application flow)
- Native mobile apps (iOS/Android) — initial scope is responsive web only
- Multi-language support (flagged as future enhancement)
- Real-time chat between buyer and broker (flagged as future enhancement)

## 7. Assumptions

- The initial launch targets a limited set of Indian cities (not a nationwide launch on day one) to keep locality data (schools/metro/hospital via Overpass API) and finance rate tables (stamp duty/registration, which are state-specific) manageable in scope.
- Builders/Brokers are willing to go through a manual verification step (document submission) before publishing — this is treated as a trust feature, not friction to eliminate.
- Payment/transaction settlement will remain off-platform (outside a bank/registrar) for the foreseeable roadmap; the platform's business value is in discovery, qualification, and connection — not in being a payments processor.
- Open-source/non-paid notification infrastructure (self-hosted SMTP, SMS gateway) is acceptable for the current phase; this may be revisited if reliability/scale requirements grow (a real business would likely migrate to a paid transactional email/SMS provider at scale).

## 8. Constraints

- **Budget:** Built as a portfolio/demo-scale project — infrastructure choices (open-source notification stack, free-tier cloud services where feasible) are constrained by non-paid/low-cost tooling, not enterprise budget.
- **Timeline:** Development roadmap (~13-16 weeks per the PRD) constrains how much of the full scope ships in v1 vs. is deferred — see MVP cut guidance in the PRD, Section 10.
- **Team size:** Single-developer build (or small team), which shapes phased delivery rather than parallel-tracked development across many features at once.
- **Legal/Regulatory:** Real estate transactions in India involve state-specific stamp duty/registration rules and RERA compliance for builders — the platform provides *estimates and verification status*, not legal or regulatory guarantees, and should be presented as such to avoid implying legal certification it doesn't provide.

## 9. Business Success Criteria

| Business Goal | Success Indicator |
|---|---|
| Buyer engagement/retention on-platform | Buyers use Finance Suite + Compare + Wishlist without dropping to external tools (trackable via Search History/analytics) |
| Broker/Builder adoption of lead pipeline | Leads are actively moved through pipeline stages rather than left static in "New Lead" |
| Listing trust/quality | Low rate of admin-rejected listings over time (indicates verification is filtering bad actors upstream) as verification/moderation matures |
| Platform health visibility | Admin can answer "how is the platform doing this month" from the Platform Analytics Dashboard alone, without manual data pulls |
| Technical credibility (portfolio goal) | The system demonstrates RBAC, caching, async pipelines, containerized deployment, and monitoring — defensible in a technical interview walkthrough |

## 10. Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Fake/fraudulent listings slip through moderation | Erodes buyer trust, the platform's core differentiator | Verification gating (Section 5.15) + duplicate-detection assist + reason-coded rejections create multiple checkpoints, not a single point of failure |
| Self-hosted email/SMS notification reliability issues | Missed booking/lead notifications hurt broker/builder trust in the platform | In-app notification is the guaranteed primary channel (Section 5.11); email/SMS explicitly scoped as best-effort, not the single point of failure |
| Scope creep beyond MVP timeline | Delivery slips past the portfolio/demo timeline | MVP cut list explicitly defined in the PRD (Section 10) — features are deferrable, not all-or-nothing |
| State-specific finance data (stamp duty, GST rules) going stale | Buyers see inaccurate estimates, damaging trust in the Finance Suite | Rate tables stored as admin-configurable data (`Finance_Rates`), not hardcoded — correctable without a redeploy |
